Our Mission

We believe in the transformative power of spiritual community — that when we gather with intention, lives change.

For over four decades, the New Thought Center of Hawaiʻi has offered a space where seekers of all backgrounds find connection, healing, and a deeper understanding of themselves and the universe.

Rooted in New Thought principles, we offer Sunday services, workshops, speaker events, and a vibrant community directory connecting practitioners across the islands.
