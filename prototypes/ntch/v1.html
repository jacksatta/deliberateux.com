Sunday Services · 10:00 AM · In-person & Livestream

"The infinite intelligence within us is always moving toward healing, wholeness, and good."

Founded in the New Thought tradition, our center brings together seekers, practitioners, and teachers who believe in the power of consciousness to transform lives.

Whether you're new to these ideas or deepening a lifelong practice, you belong here. Join us for Sunday services, workshops, and a thriving community that meets you where you are.
