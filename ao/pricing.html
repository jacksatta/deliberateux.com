Common questions

What counts as an agent run?

Any flow execution or scheduled agent tick. Starting a flow with six agents counts as six runs, not one. Failed and cancelled runs don't count.

What happens when I hit my Free limit?

Free plan runs are hard-capped at 100 / month. New runs return a friendly upgrade prompt until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade to Pro.

How does overage work on Pro and Team?

Overage is metered at $0.02 per run past your included allotment and reported to Stripe once per day. You can cap overage in org settings at any time.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade any time from your account. Annual billing switches to monthly on the next renewal; upgrades are prorated immediately.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan requires no credit card. You can keep using it indefinitely — or even continue in guest mode until you're ready to sign in.

Where does my data live?

Your org configuration, flows, and run history are scoped to your account and isolated per org. Your sandbox stays your sandbox — nothing is shared across users.
